Seven families of factors

Each factor becomes a field on a plant or plot record, so the planner can check it automatically.

Site

Hardiness zone, frost dates, season length, sun hours, day length, heat, microclimate, and indoor light and humidity.

Soil

Texture, drainage, pH, N-P-K, organic matter, salinity, compaction, and soil temperature for germination.

Planting

Direct sow or transplant, pot size, sowing depth, spacing, and how far roots spread.

Care

Moisture target and critical watering stages, pruning style, and support type (stake, cage, trellis).

Lifecycle and harvest

Annual or perennial, days to maturity, single or repeat harvest, harvest window, and succession sowing.

Neighbors

Companions, antagonists, shared diseases, and shade that changes with height and season, as in Three Sisters timing.

Pests and diseases

Photos of each affliction at its early, mid and late stage, with what causes it and how to prevent it.

Roadmap

  1. Finalize the factor and field list.
  2. Load seed data for 30–50 common vegetables, herbs and houseplants.
  3. Write tool review pages, starting with moisture meters, soil thermometers and pH meters.
  4. Build a 2D placement checker with a season slider (prototype above).
  5. Add an isometric 3D view with distinct plant silhouettes, inspired by farming sims.
  6. Build the pest and disease library with photos for each stage.
